# NoCode StartUp

Brazil's largest AI and NoCode school.

We teach non-technical professionals to build with AI: AI agents, automations, apps, SaaS and micro-SaaS. No coding required.

The school was founded in 2023 by Matheus Castelo and Neto Camarano. Today we have over 10,000 students on the platform, our content has reached more than 5 million people, and our YouTube channel has passed 160,000 subscribers.

## What's on this GitHub

Sample projects, templates and ready-to-use flows from our courses. What we build in class gets versioned here, open for the community to use and adapt.

## What you can learn at NoCode StartUp

AI agents and automations with n8n, OpenClaw and the WhatsApp and Instagram APIs. Building apps and SaaS with vibe coding tools like Claude Code, Cursor and Lovable. Backend with Supabase. Always hands-on, from zero to advanced.
